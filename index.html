About me

I came to data from the business side. For six years I managed sales teams and the reports that kept them on target.

At a real-estate group I built the management reports: leads, lead sources, conversion rates and KPIs by employee, team and project. Before that I set up and led a ten-person sales team, where I tracked daily performance in Excel and used the numbers to coach people.

That work showed me how much a clear number helps a manager decide. I trained as a data analyst to do it properly, with SQL, Python and Power BI. I bring the analysis and the business sense to know which findings matter.
